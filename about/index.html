Why AlphaCode exists

Useful development tools should help people act on ideas—not force them into one model, workflow, editor or subscription. AlphaCode combines terminal-first development, agentic workflows, model choice and automation in an MIT-licensed project.

Its goal is straightforward: make it easier to understand code, complete difficult engineering tasks and retain control over the result.

The principles

◇

Open by default

Public source code and an MIT license help developers inspect and build on the project.

✳

Freedom of choice

Use the included free AI or configure your preferred supported model and tools.

⌘

Do the work

Go beyond chat with edits, tests, browser and desktop workflows.

✓

Earn trust

Review changes and outcomes, and leave important decisions with the developer.
